## Rider benefits to say plainly

- Approval and eligibility are server-controlled; self-registration alone is not enough.
- Automatic assignment happens only after authoritative payment confirmation and rider availability.
- Product labels, manual fallback, batch progress and warnings support pickup accuracy.
- Customer-tracked GPS is consent-gated, freshness-checked and hides personal details.
- Secure POD uses in-app customer code plus a validated delivery photo.
- Earnings are posted only after verified delivery; pending payout is not paid payout.

## Editing notes

- Use the branded T-shirt only in AI ILLUSTRATIVE shots; do not imply real staff endorsements.
- Website 1920x1080; social 1080x1920 with large captions.
- Keep demo code `123456` on screen long enough to read but label it “demo code, not sent externally”.
- Do not show a bank, UTR, payout “success”, or cash amount as received.
- Captions: `public/video/subtitles/delivery-partner.<lang>.vtt`.

## Claims to avoid

- “Guaranteed ₹80,000 per month.” The figure is only a labelled hypothetical feasibility calculation in `docs/DELIVERY_PAYOUTS_DESIGN.md`.
- “Instant payout” or “payment sent”.
- “Real-time traffic ETA” unless configured and tested.
- Fake rider counts, fake ratings, guaranteed orders, or guaranteed hours.
- SMS/email OTP delivery — the implemented OTP is authenticated in-app only.
