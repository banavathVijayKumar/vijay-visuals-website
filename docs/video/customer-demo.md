## Customer benefits to say plainly

- One place for chicken, groceries, food, clothing and more.
- Price and delivery charge shown before payment.
- Cashfree-only checkout; this demo never makes a real payment.
- Step-by-step tracking with an estimated range, not a promise.
- Delivery code is generated inside the authenticated customer account; no external OTP is sent in this demo.
- Delivery proof uses the rider’s one-time code entry plus photo.

## Editing notes

- Website 1920x1080: keep on-screen text at least 42 px; safe margin 120 px.
- Social 1080x1920: reframe to center subject, burn in the same subtitles at 56–64 px, keep logo in top 15%.
- Transitions: soft cross-dissolve between real screens; quick whip-pan between product categories; no flashy cuts over payment fields.
- Music: warm, light, non-licensed-risk instrumental bed; add soft paper/notification SFX on order events.
- Captions: use `public/video/subtitles/customer.<lang>.vtt` — 8 condensed narration cues aligned to `videoShowcase.ts` (ending at 100s); the scene table above remains the full 14-scene storyboard ending at 95s.
- Do not show real names, phone numbers, addresses, order numbers other than `VV-DEMO-0001`, or real payment IDs.

## Claims to avoid

- “Guaranteed 20-minute delivery.”
- “Live traffic ETA” unless a routing service is actually configured and tested.
- Fake ratings, reviews, testimonials, customer counts, or savings figures.
- Any suggestion that SMS/email OTP delivery is configured.
