# Shop Owner Work Demo — production script and storyboard

Working title: **“Your shop, online in minutes.”**
Audience: small shop owners. Primary language: Telugu. Target length: 95 seconds.

Legal/visual label: **REAL PRODUCT SCREEN** captures must use local Preview demo data only. **AI ILLUSTRATIVE** shots must display “Illustrative scene — not a product screen”. If a workflow is not implemented, the video says so on screen.

## Shop-owner benefits to say plainly

- New orders arrive in one dashboard; no separate inbox needed.
- Accept or reject using the real status control.
- Items, quantities, payment status and price are visible before preparation.
- Product QR/Code 39 labels support pickup checking. Wrong/extra scans warn the rider.
- Sales, orders and rider commission are visible in reports.
- Weight entry and automatic weight-based price adjustment are **not** claimed as available.

## Editing notes

- Keep the weighing-scale shot clearly labelled illustrative; never show it as an app screen.
- Website 1920x1080, social 1080x1920. Reframe dashboard tables to show only 3–4 rows for legibility.
- Blur or replace all demo customer details. Use `VV-DEMO-0001` only.
- Use warm shop lighting, close-ups of hands packing, and one stable gimbal walk toward the counter.
- Captions: `public/video/subtitles/shop-owner.<lang>.vtt`.

## Claims to avoid

- “Automatic weight pricing” or “AI weighing”.
- “Instant owner payout”.
- “Order-level parcel barcode already available”.
- Fake sales totals, fake shop counts, or guaranteed income.
